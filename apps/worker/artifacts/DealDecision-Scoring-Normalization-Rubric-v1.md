# DealDecision AI - Scoring Normalization Rubric v1

## Purpose
This rubric standardizes all 1-10 scoring across audit prompts so results are comparable across deals, sectors, and analysts.

## Global Scale

### 1
Critically flawed, unsupported, or contradicted by evidence.

### 2
Very weak. Major structural issues or missing evidence. High likelihood of failure.

### 3
Weak. Some logic present, but major gaps or dependencies make confidence low.

### 4
Below average. Plausible in parts, but material weaknesses remain unresolved.

### 5
Mixed/neutral. Some strengths, some weaknesses, not yet investment-grade confidence.

### 6
Moderately solid. Reasonable logic and some support, but still meaningfully unproven.

### 7
Strong. Good evidence base, manageable risks, and coherent execution path.

### 8
Very strong. Well supported, low-moderate uncertainty, strong comparative position.

### 9
Excellent. Highly validated, highly consistent, and difficult to materially challenge.

### 10
Exceptional. Best-in-class evidence, clarity, defensibility, and execution confidence.

---

## Scoring Rules
For every score assigned, include:
- Score
- Evidence
- Main justification
- Why this was not scored one point higher
- Key missing validation item

---

## Category-Specific Interpretation

### Financial Risk Score
- 1-2: economics broken, unrealistic projections, severe hidden risk
- 3-4: major weaknesses in burn, CAC, margin, or assumptions
- 5-6: viable but fragile, important assumptions unproven
- 7-8: financially coherent with manageable risk
- 9-10: highly robust, well-supported, resilient economics

### GTM Feasibility Score
- 1-2: channel unlikely to work
- 3-4: high friction, weak evidence of repeatable acquisition
- 5-6: plausible GTM but not yet repeatable
- 7-8: strong channel logic and evidence of scalability
- 9-10: highly repeatable, efficient, validated GTM motion

### Moat Score
- 1-2: no defensibility
- 3-4: weak moat, easily copied
- 5-6: some differentiation but durability unclear
- 7-8: meaningful defensibility with evidence
- 9-10: strong durable moat, difficult to replicate

### Execution Risk Score
- 1-2: extreme execution fragility, likely failure path
- 3-4: major dependencies or operational weakness
- 5-6: real complexity, but potentially manageable
- 7-8: execution plan is strong with limited critical failure points
- 9-10: unusually high confidence in execution capability

### Interpretation Confidence Score
- 1-2: model cannot be trusted
- 3-4: major unknowns or reconstruction gaps
- 5-6: partial confidence, several assumptions unresolved
- 7-8: solid reconstructed logic with identifiable sensitivities
- 9-10: highly credible model logic with minimal ambiguity
